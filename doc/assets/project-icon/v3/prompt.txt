Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: original standalone project logo, transparent PNG, for "CLI Worker Now", a DeepSeek Harness plugin that delegates work to multiple external CLI agents and lets people SEE their live conversations in a sidebar. The mark must express visible conversations and one-to-many collaboration, not a generic coding terminal.
Primary request: Design ONE polished original, distinctive software brand SYMBOL. Compact near-square, optically balanced, confident geometric form, unusually simple and memorable enough for a 24px sidebar icon.

Specific visual concept: "An open conversation window exposing several live conversations."
ONE bold continuous rounded open-C-shaped conversation/window outline on the left creates the primary silhouette. On the right side of that open frame, arrange THREE short, strong horizontal rounded conversation strips, one above another, forming the visual impression of a sidebar containing multiple agent sessions. These three strips are NOT lines of microscopic text; they are substantial, recognizable structural parts of the logo. Unify the frame and strips into a designed emblem with purposeful aligned gaps, not a literal screenshot, hierarchy chart or disconnected clipart collage. Use a small detached circular blue status dot at the outer end of the MIDDLE conversation strip to suggest the currently live worker. The shared frame represents the parent conversation; the parallel strips are the child conversations; the opening expresses transparency/visibility. A very subtle speech-bubble inflection in one corner is allowed if it strengthens the emblem, but avoid extra decorative parts.

Style: professional flat geometric identity, soft-square corners with consistent radii, moderate bold stroke/fill mass, perfectly clean silhouettes, refined proportions. Strong negative space. Use just two flat solid blue tones: DeepSeek-compatible #4176E6 primary and #77A7FA supporting tone. No gradients, no depth, no gloss, no shadows, no black or white app tile. Fully opaque symbol fills; only clean antialiasing at contours.
Composition: ONE standalone centered logo on a square canvas, occupies roughly 78-82% of canvas, with even transparent outer margins. Background genuinely transparent alpha, internal empty space transparent, no painted checkerboard, no haze or stray particles.
Critical constraints: DO NOT use >_, chevrons, underscore, terminal command glyphs, flower/cloud/scalloped shapes, knots, robot heads, sparkle stars, the Codex brand, the DeepSeek whale, a literal letter W, overlapping two generic chat bubbles, or a node-and-wire organization chart. Previous designs were rejected for resembling Codex; this must have an independent open-frame/parallel-dialogue identity.
No wordmark, no text, no mockup board, no multiple alternatives, no annotations. Output only the finished transparent symbol.
