工具：内置 imagegen。透明背景。

## 第一轮：圆点与胶囊竖杆

Use case: precise-object-edit
Input image 1 is the user's current logo. The user is VERY SATISFIED with the entire LEFT OUTER FRAME and the tall solid CENTER CAPSULE. These are protected: preserve them in place, same contours, size, color and fill appearance. Do not redesign, recenter, rescale, or recolor them. Keep the same square transparent canvas.

Edit ONLY the rightmost two-hole shape. Replace it with a beautifully balanced, clearly recognizable lowercase "i" built of TWO SOLID SEPARATE pieces:
- a small sky-blue circular dot at the top;
- a substantially taller blue solid vertical capsule stem underneath.
There must be NO holes, enclosing rim, connection between dot and stem, serifs, feet or additional ornament on the i.

The original hollow column is too wide and top-heavy for this role. Make the new i narrower and give the dot far less visual weight than the long stem. The stem should be approximately two-and-a-half times the dot's height. The dot's diameter should be optically slightly smaller than the stem width, so it feels like a light accent above a stable upright. Both are horizontally centered on one vertical axis in the existing right-column area. The top of the dot aligns optically with the top of the center capsule; the bottom of the stem aligns optically with the bottom of the center capsule. Use the existing sky blue for the dot and the existing main blue for the stem.
Maintain a clear transparent interval between dot and stem, perceptually similar to the surrounding clearances. Preserve breathing space to the center capsule and top/bottom frame. The stem has smooth full pill ends, harmonizing with the center capsule, and the dot is smoothly circular. Match optical softness, not an arbitrary identical corner radius. The result should feel like one coherent family of bold, friendly geometric solids.

Design direction is informed by the basic separate-dot / rounded-stem typography principle, not copied from any other brand. A standalone i should be instantly readable at small size; avoid the previous 8-like double-loop shape, and avoid two squat stacked blocks that read as a colon. Keep ALL parts of the logo outside the right-column edit area unchanged. No text labels, grid, comparison board, shadows, new gradients or decorations. One clean transparent PNG logo with genuine alpha background and clear gaps.

## 第二轮：加粗并调整位置

Use case: precise-object-edit
Two inputs:
Image 1 is the user's BASE logo. Its entire left outer frame and solid central capsule are approved and MUST be preserved unchanged in appearance, shape, placement, scale and color.
Image 2 is an initial proposed RIGHT-HAND i direction. Use ONLY its dot-and-capsule idea, not changes elsewhere.

Deliver a refined version of Image 1 that changes ONLY the right-hand hollow double-loop into a SOLID lowercase i: a sky-blue round dot above a long blue capsule stem, with a transparent separation. No holes or enclosing outline. The user wants this i to belong naturally to the existing bold rounded geometric logo.

Refine the initial i from Image 2:
- It is too thin and sits too far right. Make the stem about one-quarter wider, and move the whole i slightly left. The apparent gap between the center capsule and the i should be close to the gap between the left frame and the center capsule. Avoid a large empty vertical moat.
- Make the dot slightly smaller in apparent width than the stem, but enough visual mass to balance it. Center it optically over the stem. The dot must stay distinctly shorter than the stem; a clear lowercase i rather than a colon.
- Use a generous visible dot-to-stem gap that feels consistent with the other interior spaces. The current tight slit should be opened a little.
- Align the top of the dot and bottom of the stem optically with the top and bottom of the existing central capsule. Keep clear gaps to the upper and lower outer bars.
- Retain the rounded full capsule ends of the stem and a near-circular dot. Optical curve softness should harmonize with the center capsule, not mechanically copy its radius.

Preserve Image 1's existing main blue fill and use its sky blue for the dot. Lock every part outside the right inner column. Keep the original left-projecting bottom foot and frame curvature. No rescaling of the whole logo or canvas, no extra text, no shadows, no new styling. Clean smooth boundaries without colored specks. Genuine transparent PNG with transparent background and every gap. One logo, no comparison sheet.
